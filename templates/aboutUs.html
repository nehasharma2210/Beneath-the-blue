About Beneath the Blue

We are a dedicated team of ocean conservationists working to protect marine ecosystems and inspire global stewardship of our blue planet.

Our Mission

At Beneath the Blue, we believe that the ocean's wonders should be preserved for future generations. Our mission is to educate, inspire, and mobilize people around the world to protect marine ecosystems and promote sustainable practices.

Founded in 2020, our organization brings together marine biologists, conservationists, educators, and technology experts to create innovative solutions for ocean conservation. Through research, advocacy, and community engagement, we're working to address the most pressing threats facing our oceans today.
